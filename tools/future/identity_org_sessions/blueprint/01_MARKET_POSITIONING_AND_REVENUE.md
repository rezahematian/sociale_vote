# 01 — Market Positioning and Revenue

## Market evidence reviewed on 2026-08-21

Current public competitor pricing confirms that organizations already pay for live polling,
surveying, meeting voting, reporting and managed election support:

- Poll Everywhere Business: free tier, $10/month Present, $49/month Engage and $84/month Teams when
  billed annually; paid tiers add larger audiences, reporting, registered participants and branding.
- Poll Everywhere one-time Events Lite is $499 for 1,000 participants; larger event packages are
  substantially more expensive.
- LimeSurvey Cloud lists Basic around €34/month, Expert around €374/year and Business around
  €711/year, with higher response, branding and team limits.
- ElectionBuddy distinguishes informal/standard elections from meeting votes. MeetingVote is
  approximately $197 for up to 75 voters, $397 for up to 350, and $797+ for professional tiers.
  Setup review and expert setup are separately monetized.

Conclusion: there is a real willingness to pay for **meeting workflow, participant control, reporting,
branding and support**, not merely for asking a multiple-choice question.

## Social Vote differentiation

Do not compete head-on as a slide-presentation polling clone.

Positioning:
> "Verified organizations can run simple, privacy-conscious meeting decisions without forcing every
> participant to register, then keep the result private, archive it, export it, or publish the
> aggregate outcome to their civic profile."

Key differentiators:
- no participant account required;
- optional anonymous token pool;
- verified organization context;
- multilingual IT/DE/EN;
- civic/public layer already present;
- controlled retention/deletion;
- aggregate-publication bridge;
- local support/pilot in Merano;
- later governance-grade path.

## Revenue lines

1. Annual organization plans — preferred recurring revenue.
2. One-off Session passes — useful for occasional associations.
3. Managed setup / live support — high-margin human service.
4. Public Consultation campaigns — later.
5. Team/SSO/API packages — later.
6. Governance-grade voting — only after legal and cryptographic redesign.

## Unit-economics direction

Annual billing should be the default:
- association usage is often episodic;
- it lowers payment fixed-fee drag;
- it reduces churn and monthly administration;
- organizations budget annually.

Current Stripe standard EEA card pricing is 1.5% + €0.25 per successful payment; Stripe Billing
pay-as-you-go is 0.7% of Billing volume. Those costs are small compared with a €119–€599 annual
subscription, but support and sales time will matter much more in the first year.

## Go-to-market

Merano pilot:
1. Recruit 5–10 associations with different sizes.
2. Give Verified Organization + Pro free for 90 days.
3. Personally onboard them.
4. Require at least two real Sessions, not demos.
5. Measure:
   - session creation completion;
   - join success rate;
   - participant completion;
   - time saved versus hand/show-of-hands/forms;
   - repeat use;
   - support minutes per Session;
   - willingness to pay at €119 / €249 / one-off event.
6. Convert only after the workflow is stable.

The best initial sales message is operational, not ideological:
"Run a meeting vote in 2 minutes. No accounts for participants. Choose when results appear. Keep it,
export it, or delete it."
