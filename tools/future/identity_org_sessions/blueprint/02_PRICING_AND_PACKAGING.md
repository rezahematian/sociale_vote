# 02 — Recommended Launch Pricing and Packaging

**Status:** pricing hypothesis for validation, not a binding tariff.

Displayed B2B prices below are recommended list prices **before VAT where applicable**. Customer tax
classification must be resolved before checkout/invoicing.

## Verified Organization — €0

Verification is a trust decision, not a commercial SKU.

Includes:
- verified public organization identity;
- official public profile;
- normal Social Vote Post/Poll features;
- 1 organization owner;
- Sessions Free.

Never includes:
- paid ranking;
- moderation preference;
- extra vote weight.

## Sessions Free — €0

- 2 Sessions/month;
- up to 25 participants/session;
- up to 10 questions;
- Sì/No / one answer / multiple answers;
- open anonymous;
- controlled anonymous token pool;
- 7-day raw Session retention;
- Social Vote branding;
- aggregate on-screen results;
- no CSV/PDF export.

Purpose: acquisition + proof of product value.

## Organization Starter — €119/year

Best default for small associations.

- up to 100 participants/session;
- 12 Sessions/year;
- 3 organization operators;
- token pool;
- result visibility controls;
- CSV aggregate export;
- organization logo;
- 90-day archive;
- email support;
- optional aggregate publication to Social Vote.

No monthly version at launch unless customers explicitly demand it.

## Sessions Pro — €249/year or €24.90/month

- up to 300 participants/session;
- Sessions under fair-use policy;
- 5 operators;
- controlled anonymous token pool;
- CSV + PDF aggregate reports;
- 12-month archive;
- session branding;
- result visibility/live controls;
- reusable question templates;
- scheduling;
- priority email support;
- optional aggregate publication.

## Sessions Team — €599/year or €59.90/month

- up to 1,000 participants/session;
- 15 operators;
- concurrent Sessions;
- extended configurable archive;
- advanced aggregate reports;
- priority support;
- future SSO;
- future API/webhooks;
- onboarding call.

## One-off Event Pass

For associations that do not want a subscription.

- Event 100: €29
- Event 500: €69
- Event 1,500: €149

One Session, multiple questions/motions, short archive, aggregate export.

## Human-service add-ons

- Setup Review: €149/event
- Managed Setup: €299/event
- Managed Live Session: from €399/event

These are attractive because the market already charges separately for setup/review/managed voting,
and they monetize support expertise without changing vote weight or trust.

## Future product — Social Vote Consultations

Not needed for first launch.

Potential one-off packages:
- Consultation Basic: from €99
- Consultation Pro: from €199
- Managed consultation: quote

Possible features:
- public consultation landing page;
- IT/DE/EN questions;
- location/scope;
- moderation;
- aggregate report;
- closing report;
- optional publication on verified public profile.

Do not sell paid feed reach. The paid element is tooling, hosting, workflow and reporting.

## Persona L2 pricing

Recommendation:
- launch L2 free while volume is small;
- cap retries to prevent cost abuse;
- do not make approval conditional on payment;
- if direct verification costs become material, introduce only transparent cost recovery after legal
  review, e.g. €1.99–€2.49 per successful re-verification, not a recurring fee.

Stripe Identity currently advertises €1.25 per successful document+selfie verification and the first
50 verifications free. Provider cost may change; pricing must be read at implementation time.

## Discount policy

Good:
- founding-customer annual discount;
- nonprofit/volunteer discount;
- volume discount;
- sponsored pilot by a municipality/foundation, if neutral.

Bad:
- discount in exchange for favorable public content;
- payment for badge approval;
- payment for preferential moderation;
- payment for political/civic content ranking.
