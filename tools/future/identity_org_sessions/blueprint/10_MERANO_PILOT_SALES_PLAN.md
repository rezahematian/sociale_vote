# 10 — Merano Pilot Sales Plan

## Goal

Find product-market evidence before billing engineering.

## Pilot cohort

5–10 organizations:
- one sports association;
- one cultural association;
- one volunteer/ETS organization;
- one neighborhood/community group;
- one small business/team;
- optionally one cooperative/federation.

Avoid first pilot:
- legally binding board elections;
- union votes;
- employee disciplinary/performance decisions;
- minors/schools;
- health/special-category membership votes unless privacy counsel approves.

## Offer

"Founding Merano Organization"
- verification: free;
- Sessions Pro: free for 90 days;
- onboarding: free;
- direct support;
- in return: permission to collect product feedback, not ballot data.

## Demo

15-minute demo:
1. create question;
2. project QR;
3. 5 people join without accounts;
4. vote;
5. show controlled results;
6. close;
7. export aggregate;
8. show automatic deletion setting.

## Discovery questions

Ask the president/secretary:
- How do you vote today?
- How many meetings/year?
- How many participants?
- Which decisions must be secret?
- Do you need to prove eligibility?
- Do you need quorum/proxy voting?
- Do you need signed minutes?
- Would you use it if attendees need no account?
- Which report do you need after the meeting?
- How long should data remain?
- Who operates the session?
- What would make €119/year obviously worthwhile?
- Would you prefer €29–€69 per event?

Do not lead with "Would you pay €249?" before seeing their workflow.

## Success metrics

Product:
- >90% participant join success without assistance;
- median join-to-vote under 60 seconds;
- no duplicate-vote incidents;
- organizer can run second Session without developer intervention;
- deletion/export settings understood.

Commercial:
- ≥60% run a second real Session;
- ≥30% state concrete willingness to buy;
- at least 3 convert after pilot;
- support time <30 minutes per normal Session after onboarding.

Privacy:
- no participant asks "can the organizer see my vote?" without the UI making the answer clear;
- no raw voter identity data needed in anonymous mode.

## Sales sequence

1. Personal pilot outreach.
2. On-site/remote onboarding.
3. Free 90-day use.
4. 30-day review.
5. 60-day pricing conversation.
6. 90-day conversion:
   - Starter €119/y
   - Pro €249/y
   - one-off event if subscription unsuitable.

Do not spend on ads before repeat-use evidence.
