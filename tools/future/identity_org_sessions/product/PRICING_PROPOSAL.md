# Social Vote — Proposed Organization Pricing

Status: commercial proposal for pilot validation, not yet wired into billing.

## Verified Organization
€0

Organization verification establishes authenticity. It is not the paid product.

## Sessions Free
€0
- verified organization required
- 2 live sessions/month
- up to 25 participants/session
- up to 10 questions/session
- open anonymous + controlled anonymous access
- 7-day archive
- Social Vote branding
- no CSV/PDF export

## Sessions Pro
€19.90/month or €199/year
- up to 250 participants/session
- unlimited sessions subject to fair-use limits
- up to 5 organization operators
- CSV/PDF results export
- 12-month archive
- organization logo
- configurable result visibility
- controlled anonymous token batches

## Sessions Team
€49.90/month or €499/year
- up to 1,000 participants/session
- up to 15 organization operators
- concurrent sessions
- extended archive
- custom branding
- advanced reports
- priority support
- future API/webhook access when available

## One-off event pass
Optional after pilot:
- €14.90: one event, up to 100 participants
- €39.90: one event, up to 500 participants

## Level 2 Persona
Do not use Level 2 as a profit center.
If document verification is outsourced and has a direct cost, Social Vote may later charge only a transparent cost-recovery fee. Approval must never be purchasable.

## Pilot recommendation
- First 5–10 Merano organizations: Verified Organization + Sessions Pro free for 90 days.
- Measure session creation, participant completion, repeat usage and support burden.
- Start charging only when the workflow is stable and organizations confirm that the product saves them time.
