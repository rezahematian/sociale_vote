SOCIAL VOTE — PILOT PRIVACY / BUSINESS READINESS — 2026-08-23

STATUS
Internal operational reference. This is not a signed DPA, legal opinion, or substitute for professional legal review.

CURRENT PRODUCT BOUNDARIES
- Social Vote Community: adult users (18+), Pulse/Pulse Now, Vote, Voce, News, Civic Map, profiles/follow.
- Organizations: separate Organization entity managed by authorised human accounts through memberships.
- Business pilot: Organization Workspace + Sessions + Verified Result. Billing remains OFF.
- Sessions are consultation/participation tools, not official elections, statutory votes, notarial certification, or legally binding election systems.

SESSION DATA-MINIMISATION MODEL
- Open Anonymous: no account or Access Pass; duplicate prevention is best-effort and does not guarantee one-person-one-vote.
- Controlled Anonymous: one-time Access Pass; plaintext is shown at generation time, backend stores the hash needed for validation.
- Participant technical credential is separate from the ballot.
- Ballot does not contain participant identity, Access Pass, or participant credential fields linked to the choice.
- Organizer receives aggregate results rather than identity-to-choice mappings.
- Raw ballot retention options: 24h / 7d / 30d.
- Verified Result is an immutable aggregate snapshot with Report ID and SHA-256 integrity information.
- Organizer Only reports are not publicly verifiable.

BEFORE THE FIRST EXTERNAL ORGANIZATION PILOT WITH REAL PARTICIPANTS
1. Record the Organization identity, contact, authorised representative, and pilot purpose.
2. Record the Session purpose, participant population, questions, result visibility, and chosen retention.
3. Avoid special-category data (GDPR Art. 9), minors, employee monitoring, and high-risk/sensitive use cases in the initial pilot unless separately assessed.
4. Confirm the participant notice is visible before participation and links to the full Privacy Policy.
5. Assess privacy roles for the concrete use case. Do not assume every Organization Session has the same controller/processor allocation.
6. If Social Vote processes personal data on behalf of an Organization acting as controller, determine whether an Article 28 data-processing agreement is required and execute it before the relevant processing.
7. Keep billing/payment OFF until commercial Terms, pricing, entitlements, tax/invoicing and required notices are ready.
8. Organization verification remains separate from payment; a verified badge is not sold.

GO / NO-GO FOR PILOT
GO only for low-risk adult consultation use cases after the concrete Organization/purpose/notice/retention/privacy-role review above.
NO-GO by default for legally binding elections/votes, minors, employee surveillance, or intentionally sensitive-category questionnaires until separately designed and reviewed.
